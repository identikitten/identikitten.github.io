This writing is a writing of fragments, and a writing of speed. This writing is constantly finding new routes of escaping, negotiating structure, pushing towards a new language beyond the ruins of the old one. This writing is bored, simultaneously under and over stimulated, anhedonic, it finds words in a spasm, a lurch, before dispersion and evasion come to eat it all. This writing tries to locate anger because it knows that joy may be close by, or something new, alive. This writing is an attempt to survive dislocation, drift, otherness. A declaration of

            confusion
                     and 
                          blurred
                                  identity
