I'm a mexican artist based in Vienna.

My practice explores the liminality of presence through the question of how bodies, faces, and identities are encoded, abstracted, and reinterpreted through technological and aesthetic structures.

I am interested in the boundaries between embodiment and mediation, familiarity and strangeness, intimacy and alienation, freedom and control. All mediums are interesting to me, and I work on a concept-first, medium-second basis. In the past, I've worked with text, electronics, textiles, and digital tools such as coding and text protocol hacking.

You can find me on Instagram.
