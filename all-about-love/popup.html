To navigate this essay, please navigate to the console log of your browser.
In most browsers you can do it through the following steps:

    1. Right click on the website or press
    Command + Option + i (Mac)
    Ctrl + Shift + i (Windows/Linux).
    2. Choose the option 'Inspect' or 'Inspect element'
    3. Navigate to the tab on the top named 'Console'
    If you can't find them, follow this guide or google "name of your browser" + how to open the console log

Please have both the console log and the browser open side by side, since things will happen in both windows. Ideally, the browser will cover about 70% of your screen, and the browser tools window will cover 30%. When typing, please type on the broswer, not the console log.
