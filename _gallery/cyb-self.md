       <h5>3D SCULPTING THROUGH A-FRAME.JS, HTML, CSS, MATERIALS FROM YOUTUBE</h5>
       <p><em>CyberSelfie</em> is a way of capturing the sensation of the selfie, of the attempt to represent yourself in the virtual ecosystem (and that therefore exists under its conditions). On this ecosystem, the body is assumed to be already disassembled.</p>
       <p>Taking Sean Cubitt's concept of the cyber natural - as that which transcends the organic - I look for ways to imagine my body in the coordinates of an infinite space as if I had traveled in computer time and the results that this trajectory would yield: a rotating torso, a flat brain, desire in the form of a sphere.</p>
       <p>On display at <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/CFyKCCFjmao/" target="_blank">Mvseo Privado</a>.</p>
