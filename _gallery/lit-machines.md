       <p>An experimental literary workshop exploring the theoretical and creative possibilities of cyberspace.</p>
       <p>We explored concepts such as crypto poetry, poetic terrorism, dream hacking (through lucid dreaming) and dived in abandonware and early virtual communities to find possibilities of recreation and reenactment.</p>
       <p>Facilitated in collaboration with <a href="https://www.instagram.com/mvseo_privado/" target="_blank">Mvseo Privado</a>.</p>
       <p>Explore the website <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20210710050733/http://www.maquinasliterarias.xyz/" target="_blank">before</a> a literary machine infected it and <a href="http://www.maquinasliterarias.xyz/" target="_blank">after</a>.</p>
