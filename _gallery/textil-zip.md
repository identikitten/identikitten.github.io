       <h5 class="collab">Collaboration with <a href="https://www.instagram.com/mvseo_privado/" target="_blank">Mvseo Privado</a>, <a href="#" target="_blank" class="indigo">.❀.✿.❀.</a> and <a href="https://www.instagram.com/e.stud.io" target="_blank">e.stud.io</a></h5>
       <p>A workshop exploring the connections between textile crafts and digital coding as interfaces of memory. We examine how these different forms of technology have shaped our relationships with information storage, retrieval, and communication throughout history.</p>
       <p>Participants explore the historical threads connecting textile production to early computing, from Jacquard's loom to core memory weaving by the "Little Old Ladies" who hand-wove the memory for the Apollo missions.</p>
