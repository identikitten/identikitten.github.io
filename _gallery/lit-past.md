       <h5>P5.JS, PAPER, FOUND BOOKS</h5>
       <p>These explorations began in the class 'Recreating the Past' at the School for Poetic Computation as post-concrete recreations of analog and electronic poems, approaching the text through new ways of presenting and experiencing it.</p>
       <p>By exploring, distorting, and setting them in motion, the poems become displaced, and the question arises: What happens if the expected way of reading a text is disrupted?</p>
       <p>This practice is a constant investigation that informs my artistic process. I want to understand how electronic and post-concrete poetry has been created in the past by recreating it with the resources at my disposal. Simultaneously, this reveals how electronic poetry has always had to use or "hack" the technology of the moment.</p>
