       <h5>PERFORMANCE WITH LIVE STREAMING</h5>
       <p>How has the concept of rest transformed as its boundaries with work have increasingly blurred? This performance created a space to question these limits through an audiovisual montage composed of live music and people resting in real-time via Zoom.</p>
       <p>It began with an open call in which we offered to pay participants for one hour of rest, with the condition that they would livestream it during our performance.</p>
