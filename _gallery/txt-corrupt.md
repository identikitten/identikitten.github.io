       <h5>PROJECTION, P5.JS, RASPBERRY PI, CANDLE AND COPPER OBJECTS</h5>
       <p>Exhibited at Museo Cabañas as part of 'Escrituras en presente continuo maa mo mí me muu,' curated by underbelly (Sandra Sánchez and Mariel Vela).</p>
       <p>This work emerges from an archive of my late grandmother's poems that I discovered in audio recordings. The process involved listening to her reading them, transcribing her words, and then subjecting them to algorithmic corruption through code.</p>
       <p>The resulting projection displays her poem transformed into an illegible form. As the webpage's underlying code continuously shifts, the projected text morphs and transforms while still containing the original poem.</p>
