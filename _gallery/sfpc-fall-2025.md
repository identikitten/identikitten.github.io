Fall 2025 at SFPC invokes the trickster as both methodology and muse, centering play, error, and subversion as vital strategies for steering technology towards solidarity and wonder. This season’s classes approach computation as a site of poetic misbehavior and political possibilities. What becomes possible when we misuse tools and question authorship?
