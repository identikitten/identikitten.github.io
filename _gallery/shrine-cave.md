       <h5 class="collab">Collaboration with Fereshteh Toosi as a final project for the Experiments of Networked Performance class</h5>
       <p>▟▘▝▗▘▗▘🥀▗▝▗▘▗▝▟▘🥀 I dream of understanding the love for death, not feeling an immense attachment to everything I love. I let all the falling flowers dry and keep them around; when they are alive, they remind me of my mother and her garden, and of the times when I dreamt of having a quiet life where we sat to watch white butterflies</p>
       <p>▟▘▝▗▘▗▘🥀▗▝▗▘▗▝▟▘🥀▝▗▘▗▘▟▘ that image torments me because it's always distant. I won't have that garden or those dreams where fear doesn't exist ▟▘▝▗▘▗▘🥀▗▝▗▘▗▝▟▘🥀▝▗▘▗▘▟▘ What will time do to my body as it does to dried flowers? Who would want to preserve what I once was, without fear of losing myself, without fear of time, without fear of the world? ▟▘▝▗▘▗▘🥀▗▝▗▘▗▝▟▘🥀▝▗▘▗▘▟▘▟▘▝▗▘▗▘🥀▗▝▗▘▗▝▟▘🥀▝▗▘▗▘▟▘</p>
       <p>On the other hand, an altar to oil or an altar to death, a reminder that we don't choose everything we are, that there's cruelty in the things we depend on, that we are made of love but also of violence▝▗▘▗▘▟▘▟▘▝▗▘▗▘🥀▗▝▗▘▗▝▟▘🥀▝▗▘▗▘▟▘</p>
       <p>I feel very honored and happy to have been able to collaborate with Fereshteh Toosi, an artist whom I admire a lot, on this piece that was part of our final exercise for Experiments in Networked Performance at the School for Poetic Computation. Here, we combined a narrative exploration with Fereshteh's work, where oil can be traced through ancestry.▟▘▝▗▘▗▘🥀▗▝▗▘▗▝▟▘🥀▝▗▘▗▘▟▘</p>
