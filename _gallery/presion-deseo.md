       <h5>In memory of <span class="hw">Horacio Warpola</span>, dear friend and collaborator, co-creator of <a href="https://www.instagram.com/sqnx.mx/" target="_blank">SQNX</a>, transmedia collective</h5>
       <h5>PERFORMANCE WITH LIVE INTERACTION (SOCKET.JS, HTML & CSS) & LIVE MUSIC</h5>
       <p>A performance about creative disenchantment where <span class="hw">Horacio Warpola</span> and I wrote about some ✨💰magical solutions 💰✨ that we ourselves cannot strictly follow, but the dream is always there. It was a farse, a game, and a way to confront internalized dreams, rid ourselves of expectations placed on an artist, and acknowledge that artistic disenchantment corresponds to productivity and discursive logics that we sometimes attend to and that make us sad.</p>
       <p>It was a collaboration with the transmedia collective SQNX, involving sound design by Rojotodoeltiempo and Jorge Pitol, and visuals and interactions with the audience through a custom-made phone controller by Isra Viadest.</p>
