       <p>A speculative and literary workshop co-facilitated with <a href="https://vdst.cargo.site" target="_blank">Israel Viadest</a> on November 2021 in <a href="https://centroculturadigital.mx/actividad/Marginalia-refractiva-Imaginar-futuros-de-Mexico-x6YuTuWPj" target="_blank">Centro de Cultura Digital</a>, Ciudad de México, where we wanted to challenge our collective relationship to technology through speculative and retro-speculative fiction.</p>
       <p>Using a custom-made set of cards, we invited participants to travel to the past, eliminating one of the two central technological machines in our history: the Jacquard's loom or the Gutenberg's press. Then we traveled into the future and imagined a world shaped by a different course of history, undetermined by one of these two machines.</p>
       <p>Participants were expected to produce prototypes, historical timelines, an artifact and a literary object by the end of the workshop.</p>
