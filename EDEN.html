Welcome to my Evolving Digital Ecologic Network.

The spirit of this page emulates the type of websites created in the early 2000s, where people compiled links, thoughts and texts as a small collection of things. I like the idea of treating hypertext as a technology of mental trinket collection, just as you do in the forest or the beach with cool little rocks and shells.
